## Slide 4: Performance Uplift: Statistically Proven
- **Welch's Two-Sample t-Test:**
  - Minimal Training Cohort ($\le 15$ hrs): **Mean Post Rating = 3.09**
  - High Training Cohort ($> 35$ hrs): **Mean Post Rating = 3.90**
  - Result: $t = 47.82, p < 0.0001, \text{Cohen's } d = 1.62$ (Massive positive effect size)
- **Correlation:** Strong positive correlation ($r = +0.58$) between training hours and post-training performance ratings.

---

## Slide 5: Behavioral Transformation (Discipline & Punctuality)
- **Attendance Days:** Rose from 208 days in low-training cohorts to **224 days** in high-training cohorts.
- **Late Arrivals:** Dropped from an average of **12.8 days/year** down to **4.6 days/year** ($r = -0.41$).
- **Insight:** Structured learning programs instill accountability and engagement, cascading directly into daily workplace punctuality.

---

## Slide 6: The "Trained Talent Flight" Anomaly
```
Cohort Attrition Rates:
- Overall Workforce:                      12.8%
- Trained & Well-Compensated Cohort:       4.2%
- High Trained, High Rated, Underpaid:    48.2% (CRITICAL RISK)
```
- **The Mechanism:** Upskilled employees with $>40$ training hours and ratings $\ge 4.0$ whose salaries lag market benchmarks get aggressively recruited by competitors.
- **Action Required:** Close the compensation gap before competitors do.
